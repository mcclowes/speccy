# Privacy

**Last updated:** August 13, 2026

Speccy documentation stores your privacy choice and display preferences in your browser. This necessary storage stays on your device and keeps the site consistent between visits.

The site sends anonymous page-view data to Vercel Web Analytics. This can include the page URL, referrer, approximate location, browser, operating system, and device type. Vercel Web Analytics does not use cookies, retain IP addresses, or track visitors across websites. Its daily visitor identifier expires after 24 hours.

Speccy does not currently use analytics cookies, marketing cookies, functional cookies, or advertising trackers. Vercel’s cookieless analytics does not depend on your cookie preference.

## Change your choice

To change your preference, remove the `speccy-cookie-consent` local storage entry through your browser’s site-data controls, then refresh the page. The privacy prompt will appear again.
